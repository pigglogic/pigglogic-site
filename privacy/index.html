Privacy policy · Updated October 31, 2026

This website

This site does not use cookies, advertising, or analytics. Fonts and images are served with the site; there are no embedded third-party trackers.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and privacy statement.

If you contact us

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your message. Please share only what is needed for your inquiry.

Our apps

This policy covers the Pigg Logic company website and correspondence with the studio. For Chordstep information, documentation, and app policies, visit chordstep.com.

Questions or changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and its date. For privacy questions, email hello@pigglogic.com.
